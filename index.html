The Essential Health ethos:
individualized care, every time.

We don't believe that courses of treatments ought to be pre-determined or dictated by a pre-defined model of practice. Rather, we work organically — taking into account all the factors that shape our individual client's lives and their consequent health.

Our team of highly educated Registered Massage Therapists provides individualized care for you. We provide the highest quality care to resolve pain, reduce the occurrence of injury, improve mobility and decrease stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Do you provide insurance receipts?
Yes. All Registered Massage Therapy sessions come with an official insurance receipt. Please check with your provider for your specific coverage details.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book online through our booking form, call us directly, or send us an email. Secure online booking with electronic health history forms — only a few clicks away.
Do I need a doctor's referral?
No referral is needed for massage therapy. However, some insurance plans may require one — check with your provider before booking.
What should I expect during my first visit?
Your therapist will review your health history and discuss your goals. Treatment is always tailored to your individual needs and comfort level.
Is parking available?
Yes. Free parking is available at our 700 Industrial Avenue location in the Riverview Park neighbourhood of Ottawa.
Do you offer bilingual services?
Yes. We offer services in both English and French. Please let us know your preference when booking your appointment.
